      How to play

      Walk with WASD or the arrow keys, or just click where you want to go. Stand in front of a door and press E to go in. On a phone, use the on-screen buttons.

      💾

      Progress is saved

      Your progress is saved in this browser, so you can come back later. You can reset it from the character sheet.
